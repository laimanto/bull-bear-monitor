Bull-Bear Monitorv1

__BUILD_TS__
One generic rule set on every market — VB (volatility breaker 45/18 — fast crashes) + GC (golden cross 50/200 — slow bears) + RC (Recovery override — early buy-back) — all-in or all-out at daily closes. Green pip = IN the market, red pip = OUT. Cash earns nothing while out; no trading costs or taxes modeled.
